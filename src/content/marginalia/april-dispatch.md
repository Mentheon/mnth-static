A quiet month, by design. The Kindreon team locked the v0.4 prop vocabulary at the start of April and we've been living inside it since: building three reference rooms (kitchen, garden bench, photograph wall) and watching what falls out. The kitchen is, predictably, the hardest. Kettles are a meaning-rich object and we keep over-engineering them.

On the writing side, the draft of the *low-arousal authoring* paper finally has a shape. It will not be done in time for the next deadline I had in mind, but it will be done in time for the one after, which is a more honest place to be. Co-authors have been generous with margins.

A visit, too: a short trip up to the partner clinical site mid-month. Two sessions observed, one of them very good and one of them clarifying in the harder way. I'll write that up properly elsewhere — there are things I want to say about the difference between a participant being engaged and a participant being polite, and I want longer than a dispatch entry to say them.

Otherwise: spring is here, the helix is rendering reliably across the new browser version, and we have a working CI pipeline for the first time in months. I will take wins where I find them.
