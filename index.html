Mentheon — a new era of digital health

Mentheon Ltd is a digital health research company. Its research programme (2024–26) runs as strands, each building software toward one clinical aim:

  • Plasmids — software
  • Detection — detect
  • Monitoring — measure
  • Therapeutics — intervene, including personalised VR reminiscence therapy for dementia care
  • Perception — understand

Advisory: research, design, development and consultancy services for software in any domain. Digital health is our focus, not our limit: every engagement is bottom-up and evidence-based, and we stay engaged across the whole research, design and development pipeline.
