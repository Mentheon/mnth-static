Aufdröselung logo, Essen and the characters.

The Aufdröselung product mark, Essen in his five states, and the recurring cast of the Essen universe. All characters are pixel art on their native grid, exported as crisp-edged SVG so they scale cleanly.

The cast

Rules

  • Render at whole-number multiples of the native grid (×2, ×4, ×8) with image-rendering: pixelated.
  • Outlines are deep plum #2F0147; don't add drop shadows or soft glows to the sprites.
  • Nick, Paula, the bunny and the bear are personal; use them only in "nur für paula" or beta-tester builds, never in public marketing.
  • Essen is the public mascot and may appear anywhere in Aufdröselung.
